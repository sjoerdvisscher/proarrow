Optic composition by graph substitution

Each source wire expands to a forward (+) and backward (−) wire. Each generator expands into a forward box and a backward box, connected by a residual m_f or m_g. The forward pass runs f then g; the backward pass runs g then f.

The encoding composes with paired boundaries. Moving the external backward wires gives inputs (a+, c−) and outputs (c+, a−). Residuals connect the two passes without recomputing them.
