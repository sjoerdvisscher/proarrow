# Revision history for proarrow

## 0.1 -- 2026-09-07

* First release: kind-indexed categories, profunctors as the central
  abstraction, monoidal structure, (co)limits, adjunctions, Kan extensions,
  enriched categories, promonads, and a full profunctor-optics hierarchy
  with subtyping. The public sublibrary `proarrow:testing` provides generic
  law-checking properties for testing your own categories.
